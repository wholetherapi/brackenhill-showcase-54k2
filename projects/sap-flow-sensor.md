## Overview

Sap Flow Sensor drives a small thermal probe and measures the temperature
difference between two thermistors clamped to a stem. The probe is intended for
orchard trees with stems between 40 and 120 millimetres in diameter.

## Field notes

Probes are installed before dawn to avoid cavitation and are insulated with a
reflective sleeve. Readings are logged every 15 minutes.
