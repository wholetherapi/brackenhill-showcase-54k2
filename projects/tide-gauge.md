## Overview

Tide Gauge fires an ultrasonic pulse at the water surface and averages a burst
of returns to estimate water level to the nearest centimetre. Readings are
stored locally and can be relayed over a low-power radio link.

## Field notes

The mast is anchored to a steel pile and the transducer sits inside a foam-lined
housing to dampen wind noise. A desiccant sachet is replaced at each service
visit.
